Legends

The great defenders of the past, from Richard Bergmann's world titles in the 1930s to the choppers who kept the style alive in the modern game.

  • Richard Bergmann

    Classical chopper

    1930s to 1950s

    World singles champion for Austria in 1937 and for England in 1939, 1948 and 1950, noted above all for his defence, from the era when defence was a route to the very top.

    Source
    • European Table Tennis Hall of FameHistoryRichard BergmannThe four-time world champion of the 1930s to 1950s, famous for his defence, and the era when defence was a route to the top.

  • Tong Ling

    Classical chopper

    1970s and 1980s

    Won the women's world singles title as a defender in Novi Sad in 1981.

    Source

  • Chen Xinhua

    Classical chopper

    1980s and 1990s

    Chinese defender who won the World Cup in 1985 and represented England from 1990.

    Source
    • WikipediaHistoryChen XinhuaThe Chinese defender who won the 1985 World Cup and later played for England.

  • Ding Song

    Modern defender

    1990s

    Chopped with backspin, then attacked with his forehand. In the 1995 men's team final in Tianjin he beat Peter Karlsson as China beat Sweden 3-2.

    Source
    • ITTFHistory7 May 2020World Championships rewind: Ding Song in TianjinHow the defender Ding Song beat Peter Karlsson in the 1995 men's team final to keep China in the tie.
    • MegaspinHistoryDefensive star wins Korean OpenJoo Se-hyuk becomes the first defender to win a men's Pro Tour singles title since Ding Song in 1997.

  • Koji Matsushita

    Classical chopper

    1990s and 2000s

    Japan's leading chopper of his generation, whose name is still on Butterfly's defensive blades used by Han Ying, Linda Bergström and Wang Yang.

    Source

  • Joo Se-hyuk

    Modern defender

    2000s and 2010s

    The defining modern men's chopper: world singles runner-up in 2003, the first defender to win a men's Pro Tour singles title since Ding Song when he took the Korean Open in 2006, and still world No 8 in 2010.

    Source

  • Kim Kyung-ah

    Classical chopper

    2000s and 2010s

    Olympic singles bronze medallist in Athens in 2004 and world No 5 in 2010.

    Source
    • WikipediaHistoryKim Kyung-ahThe Korean chopper who won Olympic bronze in Athens in 2004 and reached No 5 in the world.

  • Hou Yingchao

    Classical chopper

    2000s to 2019

    At 39, beat the young Wang Chuqin 4-0 to win the 2019 Chinese national title, 19 years after his first, the oldest national champion China has had.

    Classical chopper

    2010s

    World junior champion in 2009 whose chopping took her to No 4 in the world.

    Classical chopper

    2010s and 2020s

    Modern defender
