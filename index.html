Fang Bo wins first title for 13 years and Honoka Hashimoto takes WTT Contender Istanbul crown

Kazakhstan's Fang Bo, a wild card ranked 160th, beat Kang Dongsoo 4-1 seven months into his comeback, and the top seed Honoka Hashimoto beat Joo Cheonhui by the same score.
