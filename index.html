WTT Finals Hong Kong: Togami, Jorgic, Dang Qiu and Lin Shidong complete the men's field

WTT has confirmed all 32 singles players for the WTT Finals Hong Kong, 9 to 13 December, with Sora Matsushima top of the men's race, defending champions Tomokazu Harimoto and Wang Manyu in the field, and Joo Cheonhui and Sabine Winter taking the last women's places.
